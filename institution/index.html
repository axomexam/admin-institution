How this library works

Everything you need to know before handing these papers to a class.

How the folders are organised

The library mirrors the folder structure of the question bank: exam book, then subject, then topic. Opening ADRE Grade III shows its six subject folders — General Mathematics, General English, General Knowledge and Assam GK, History, Logical Reasoning and Social Studies. Inside a folder, each topic is a single PDF you can download on its own, so a teacher can hand out exactly one topic without printing the whole book.

What each PDF contains
  • Every question in that topic, in exam order, with four options.
  • The correct answer marked, plus the explanation — in whichever language you picked.
  • The axomexam.in watermark across the page, with the logo and site name in the top corner of every page.
  • A page number in the footer, so a printed set stays in order.
Downloading for an institution

There is no paywall and no per-seat limit. Sign in once, open a folder, click a file, pick English or অসমীয়া in the dialog, and that language's PDF downloads. Each topic is a separate file, so you take only what you need. Files are print-ready on A4 and stay readable in black and white.

Which books are available now

Two books are published today: ADRE 3.0 Grade III and ADRE 3.0 Grade IV. Other exams on the site are still being converted and will appear here as separate books when their PDFs are ready. The question data itself is always available on the practice pages, so nothing is hidden while a book is being built.

Keeping a copy up to date

A PDF is a snapshot. When the question bank gains new questions, the matching topic PDF is rebuilt and the download link serves the new file. If you archive these files for a class, re-download the topics you use at the start of each term so your copy matches the site.

About the mock tests

Alongside the PDFs there are 10 full-length mock tests — 5 sets each for ADRE Grade III and Grade IV. Every set mixes all the subjects of that paper and runs on a live timer, so a batch can sit a paper exactly as it appears in the exam hall. Pick Assamese or English before starting, use the question palette to move around, and submit to get an instant scorecard with the correct answer and an explanation for every question. Negative marking of 0.25 applies, as in the real paper.

These tests run entirely in the browser, so a whole classroom can take the same paper at the same time without any login. The scorecard prints as a PDF under the candidate's own name. They are meant for your students only and are excluded from search engines.
